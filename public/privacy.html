Privacy Policy — S_19 CHAT BOOK

Last updated: 10 November 2026

S_19 CHAT BOOK is a free messaging and calling app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: the phone number or Gmail address you log in with, and the name you choose.
  • Profile details: your profile photo and About text, if you set them.
  • Messages and media: the messages, photos, videos, voice notes and files you send in chats, and the contacts you chat with.
  • Usage details: when you were last online, and how much data the app used, so the app can work properly.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to deliver your messages and calls, to show your chats, and to keep your account working. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not show advertisements.

How messages are stored

Your messages are stored on our server so they can be delivered when you are offline, just like other messaging apps. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Only you and the person you are chatting with can open a chat inside the app, but the messages are stored on our server and are not encrypted end-to-end.

Login codes

When you log in with Gmail, we send a 6-digit login code to that Gmail address. The code is valid for 10 minutes. Never share your login code with anyone.

Deleting your data

If you want your account and your messages deleted, email us at s19chatbook@gmail.com from the Gmail address or with the phone number of your account, and we will delete them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: s19chatbook@gmail.com
