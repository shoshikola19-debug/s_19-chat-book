Help and feedback
How it works
Log in — with your phone number or Gmail. A 6-digit code confirms it is you.

Chats — tap + to add a friend by number or Gmail, then message them. Photos and videos send at original quality. Long-press a message to delete it.

Calls — tap the phone or video button in a chat. Voice calls use about 120 KB per minute; video calls use much more.

Groups — tap + then "New group", pick friends, and chat together. Any member can add more people.

Status & channels — post a status in Updates (it lasts 24 hours), or follow a channel.
Privacy policy
Messages are stored on our server (Render / Neon) so they can reach you when you come back online. They are NOT end-to-end encrypted — the server could technically read them. In the app, only you and the person you chat with can open a chat; strangers cannot. Your profile photo and name are visible to people you chat with.
